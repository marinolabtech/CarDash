CarDash Server

Privacy Policy | Version 1.1 | July 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how CarDash Server (the "Application") collects, uses, and protects your personal data. The Application acts as a local gateway to transmit information from your smartphone to a secondary tablet device (Dashboard Bridge) within the same vehicle.

Data Collection and Sensitive Permissions

To provide its core functionality, the Application requires access to the following data, which is handled exclusively for mirroring purposes on your connected Bridge device:

  • Contacts and Call Logs: Used to display caller identity and manage phone calls directly from the Dashboard tablet.
  • GPS Location (Fine/Background): Necessary for the "Parking Tracker" feature and to provide accurate telemetry to the tablet during navigation.
  • Notifications (Listener Service): Used to forward messages (e.g., WhatsApp, Telegram) to the Dashboard tablet for safer driving.
  • Phone State: Required to detect incoming calls and manage the remote answering interface.

Security and AES-256 Encryption

Your privacy is our priority. All data transmitted between the smartphone and the tablet over the local network (Wi-Fi or Hotspot) is protected by an advanced AES-256 encryption layer.

No personal data is ever sent to external servers, third parties, or Marino Lab. Communication occurs exclusively "Peer-to-Peer" within your private local network.

Data Usage

Collected data is never sold, rented, or shared. It is used solely to:

  1. Display driving information on the connected tablet.
  2. Manage phone functions remotely.
  3. Locally store the parking location for later vehicle retrieval.

User Rights

Users can revoke access to permissions at any time through Android system settings or via the dedicated activity within the app. Revoking certain permissions (e.g., Contacts or Call Logs) will disable the respective mirroring functions.
